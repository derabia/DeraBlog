# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, friendly, and respectful environment for everyone who participates in the DeraBlog project, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communications.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and any other community communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at: hello@derablog.com

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
